# Solid surfaces and compact agent layout — 20 November 2026

The user removed the previous Liquid Glass requirement. Customer, agent and
merchant navigation and selection sheets now use opaque light/dark surfaces.
The blur and glass dependencies are removed from all three apps. Existing
selection-sheet transitions, safe areas and orange actions remain.

Agent home groups headings with their content rather than spacing every history
row as a separate section. The daily summary uses a horizontal count, smaller
padding and per-currency totals. Cash shortcuts use less vertical space while
retaining touch targets. Completed operation rows show customer or wallet name, cash-in/cash-out,
compact time and amount; their references remain in the operation detail.
Pending queue rows retain their status.

Background history failures no longer display an operations refresh banner on
Home, Queue or Activity. Cached records remain visible and pull-to-refresh works.
If no history has loaded, totals show an em dash and the empty state says no
operations have loaded, rather than claiming a zero balance or clear queue.
Errors from submitted money operations still remain visible.

The follow-up density pass extends to agent More, Settings, Profile, cash forms
and confirmation details; customer More and Profile; merchant Settings and
Collections. Agent More removes payment/scan shortcuts duplicated by Home and
navigation. Settings rows retain at least a 52-point target. Primary money
buttons and form validation remain unchanged.
